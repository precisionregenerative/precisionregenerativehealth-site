Our Therapies

Orthobiologic treatment options

Orthobiologics use substances derived from your own body to stimulate healing in joints, tendons, ligaments, and cartilage. Each therapy is selected based on your condition, imaging, and clinical presentation.

PRP

Platelet-Rich Plasma

Your platelets, concentrated and precisely injected. The strongest evidence base among orthobiologics.

Learn more
BMAC

Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate

Bone marrow cells concentrated and injected to support regeneration of damaged tissue.

Learn more
MFAT

Microfragmented Adipose Tissue

Processed fat tissue rich in regenerative cells and growth factors for joints and soft tissue.

Learn more
Viscosupplementation

Hyaluronic Acid Injections

Improves joint lubrication and cushioning to reduce pain and stiffness.

Learn more
Investigational

Stem Cell–Based Therapies

Emerging therapies, discussed case by case with transparent guidance on the evidence.

Learn more
