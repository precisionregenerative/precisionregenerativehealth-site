Our Therapies

Orthobiologic Treatment Options

Orthobiologics use substances derived from your own body to stimulate healing in joints, tendons, ligaments, and cartilage. Each therapy is selected based on your specific condition, imaging, and clinical presentation.

🔬
Platelet-Rich Plasma
PRP

Blood is drawn, platelets are concentrated, and injected into the injured area. The strongest evidence base among orthobiologics.

  • Knee Osteoarthritis
  • Tennis Elbow
  • Achilles Tendinopathy
  • Rotator Cuff Injuries
🧬
Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate
BMAC

Bone marrow cells harvested from the pelvis, concentrated, and injected to support regeneration of damaged tissue.

  • Cartilage Injuries
  • Arthritis
  • Tendon & Ligament Injuries
💧
Microfragmented Adipose Tissue
MFAT

Processed fat tissue rich in regenerative cells and growth factors, used to support healing in joints and soft tissue.

  • Knee Arthritis
  • Soft Tissue Injuries
⚡
Hyaluronic Acid Injections
Viscosupplementation

A naturally occurring substance in joints that improves lubrication and cushioning, reducing pain and stiffness.

  • Knee Osteoarthritis
  • Joint Stiffness & Pain
🔩
Stem Cell–Based Therapies
Investigational

Emerging therapies under rigorous evaluation. Dr. Sridhar provides transparent guidance on where evidence stands and what is FDA-compliant.

  • Discussed case-by-case
  • With full informed consent

Important Clinical Information

Results from orthobiologic therapies vary widely depending on condition type, severity, and individual patient factors. PRP currently holds the strongest evidence base among orthobiologics for certain tendon problems and mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis. Many regenerative therapies remain under active investigation and are not considered cures. These treatments are typically not covered by insurance. Dr. Sridhar is committed to transparent, evidence-based discussions so patients can make fully informed decisions about their care.
