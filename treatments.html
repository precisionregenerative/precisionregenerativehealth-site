Our Therapies

Orthobiologic treatment options

Orthobiologics use substances derived from your own body to stimulate healing in joints, tendons, ligaments, and cartilage. Each therapy is selected based on your specific condition, imaging, and clinical presentation.

  • PRP
  • BMAC
  • MFAT
  • Hyaluronic Acid
  • Stem Cell–Based
PRP

Platelet-Rich Plasma

Blood is drawn, platelets are concentrated, and the concentrate is injected into the injured area. PRP has the strongest evidence base among orthobiologics, particularly for certain tendon problems and mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis.

Ask about PRP

Commonly considered for

  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Tennis elbow
  • Achilles tendinopathy
  • Rotator cuff injuries
BMAC

Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate

Bone marrow cells are harvested from the pelvis, concentrated, and injected to support regeneration of damaged tissue.

Ask about BMAC

Commonly considered for

  • Cartilage injuries
  • Arthritis
  • Tendon & ligament injuries
MFAT

Microfragmented Adipose Tissue

Processed fat tissue rich in regenerative cells and growth factors, used to support healing in joints and soft tissue.

Ask about MFAT

Commonly considered for

  • Knee arthritis
  • Soft tissue injuries
Viscosupplementation

Hyaluronic Acid Injections

Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring substance in joints. Injections improve lubrication and cushioning, which can reduce pain and stiffness.

Ask about hyaluronic acid

Commonly considered for

  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Joint stiffness & pain
