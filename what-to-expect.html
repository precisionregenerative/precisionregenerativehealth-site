Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are orthobiologic treatments covered by insurance?

These treatments are typically not covered by insurance. We're happy to talk through costs during your free discovery call.

Do these treatments work for everyone?

No. Results vary widely depending on condition type, severity, and individual patient factors. Orthobiologics are not considered cures, and many remain under active investigation. Dr. Sridhar will give you an honest, evidence-based view of what to expect in your case.

Which treatment is right for me?

Every patient is different. We evaluate your history, imaging, and goals to recommend the most appropriate therapy. A free discovery call is a good first step.

How are the injections performed?

Orthobiologic therapies are administered under ultrasound guidance, which allows precise placement for accuracy and safety.

Do you offer stem cell therapy?

Stem cell–based therapies are emerging and remain under rigorous evaluation. Dr. Sridhar discusses them case by case, with full informed consent and transparent guidance on where the evidence stands and what is FDA-compliant.

How do I get started?

Book a free discovery call. We'll learn about your situation and, if it makes sense, schedule a full consultation. Book your call here.
