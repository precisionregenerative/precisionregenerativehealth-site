Conditions We Treat

We specialize in musculoskeletal healing

From early-stage tendinitis to advanced osteoarthritis, we offer targeted orthobiologic options for a wide spectrum of orthopedic conditions, helping many patients avoid or delay surgery.

  • Knee
  • Shoulder
  • Elbow
  • Hip
  • Foot & Ankle
  • Tendons, Ligaments & Sports

01

Knee

Knee pain is one of the most common reasons patients explore orthobiologics, from early cartilage wear to established arthritis.

Conditions

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Meniscus injuries
  • Cartilage defects
  • Patellar tendinopathy

Therapies often discussed

  • PRP
  • BMAC
  • MFAT
  • Hyaluronic acid

02

Shoulder

Options for rotator cuff and tendon problems that limit reaching, lifting, and sleep.

Conditions

  • Rotator cuff tears & injuries
  • Tendinitis & tendinopathy

Therapies often discussed

  • PRP

03

Elbow

Overuse injuries of the elbow tendons, common in athletes and in repetitive work.

Conditions

  • Tennis elbow
  • Golfer's elbow

Therapies often discussed

  • PRP

04

Hip

Evaluation and orthobiologic options for hip pain related to impingement and joint wear.

Conditions

  • Hip impingement
  • Arthritis

Therapies often discussed

  • BMAC

05

Foot & Ankle

Persistent heel and tendon pain that hasn't settled with rest and conservative care.

Conditions

  • Achilles tendinopathy
  • Plantar fasciitis

Therapies often discussed

  • PRP

06

Tendons, Ligaments & Sports Injuries

Soft tissue injuries throughout the body, for weekend athletes and competitive athletes alike.

Conditions

  • Ligament sprains
  • Tendinitis & tendinopathy
  • Sports injuries
  • Soft tissue injuries

Therapies often discussed

  • PRP
  • BMAC
  • MFAT

The right therapy depends on your condition, its severity, your imaging, and your goals. These lists are a starting point for conversation, not a recommendation.
